# Executive summary — Phase 1

The screening reviewed 18 operational problems across manufacturing, logistics, construction, financial services, healthcare and energy. Three merit deeper investigation because customers can measure the operational outcome and test machine learning against a credible existing workflow.

| Opportunity | Why it merits a closer look | What must be demonstrated |
|---|---|---|
| Empty road freight kilometres | Eurostat reports that empty vehicles accounted for 21.8% of EU road freight vehicle-kilometres in 2025, excluding Malta. Some empty movements are operationally necessary. [Eurostat](https://ec.europa.eu/eurostat/statistics-explained/SEPDF/cache/11732.pdf?v=1832606267778350) | Combine demand or travel-time predictions with constrained dispatch. Demonstrate lower total kilometres or higher contribution margin against deterministic optimization at comparable service levels. |
| Outpatient no-shows | NHS England reported a 5.42% no-show rate across 150.3 million outpatient appointments in England in FY 2025/26. Reporting coverage varies by provider. [NHS England](https://digital.nhs.uk/data-and-information/publications/statistical/hospital-outpatient-activity/2025-26/summary-reports) | Compare targeted outreach with standard reminders and a rules-based waitlist. Measure additional completed visits, economic value the buyer can capture, and equitable access. |
| Industrial energy use above baseline | The IEA reports average energy savings above 10% within the first three years of industrial energy management programs. That benchmark captures entire programs and provides no estimate of the incremental contribution of AI. [IEA](https://www.iea.org/reports/energy-management-for-industry/executive-summary) | In plants with existing submeters, measure whether model-based alerts deliver additional normalized kWh savings over regression and engineering thresholds at comparable staff effort. |

**Decision at this stage.** Validate all three candidates. Before selecting a solution or launch market, confirm historical data access, the share of losses a customer can act on, the cost of integration and the buyer's ability to capture savings or incremental margin. The screening includes illustrative customer payback thresholds. Provider returns and an investment recommendation remain open.
